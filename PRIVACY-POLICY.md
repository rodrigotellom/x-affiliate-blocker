# X Affiliate Blocker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

X Affiliate Blocker runs entirely in your browser.

**What it reads.** On x.com and twitter.com, it reads the account data that x.com
already sends your browser (handles, display names and organization affiliation
labels) to decide which accounts match the companies you chose.

**What it stores.** In Chrome's extension storage on your device: your company list,
your settings, the handles of accounts it has blocked for you, and a tally of
organizations seen while you browse. Settings may sync between your own Chrome
profiles through Chrome Sync.

**What it sends.** Block requests to x.com, made with your logged-in session, for
accounts that match your list, and only when you turn blocking on. Nothing is
sent to the developer or any third party. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

**Removing data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rodrigotellom/x-affiliate-blocker.
