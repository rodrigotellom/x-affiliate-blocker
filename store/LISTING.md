## Detailed description

```
X Affiliate Blocker hides or blocks everyone X lists as affiliated with a company you choose: employees, executives and company-run accounts.

HOW IT WORKS

Companies on X's Verified Organizations plan link their employees' accounts to the company account. That link is the small company logo next to an employee's name, and it is included in the data x.com already sends your browser. The extension reads that link, so matching is exact rather than guessed from bios.

USING IT

1. Open the popup on x.com and add a company by handle (@company) or name.
2. By default, matching accounts' posts are just hidden from your timeline.
3. Turn on "Actually block" to block them with your own account. Blocks go out one at a time, several seconds apart, and pause automatically if X rate-limits you.
4. To catch a whole team at once, open x.com/<company>/affiliates and scroll to the bottom.

OPTIONS

- Skip people I follow (on by default)
- Pause
- Organizations seen: companies whose affiliates appeared while you browsed, with one-click add
- Recently blocked: who was blocked and which company they're linked to

PRIVACY

Everything stays in your browser. Your company list, settings and block history are saved in Chrome's extension storage. The extension makes no requests to any server other than x.com, and only to block the accounts you asked it to.

LIMITS

- It can only catch accounts the company has officially linked.
- X discourages automated mass-blocking; keep the built-in delay.
- If X changes its data format, matching may stop until the extension is updated.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.
```
